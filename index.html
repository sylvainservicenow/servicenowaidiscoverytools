AI Agent Advisor

AI Agent Advisor docs ↗

Analyzes instance data to identify automation opportunities and recommend or pre-generate AI agents.

Best use case
Use as the default starting point for agentic opportunity discovery from real customer data.
Customer-ready?
Yes, Store
License / entitlement
Available by AI/product tier; field materials say customer needs a license including Now Assist (Pro+ or new commercial model).
Overlap / caution
Preferred productized discovery tool; overlaps with Forge Mining and LEAP but is broader than LEAP and more productized than Labs.
AI-powered?
Yes
Primary domain
Cross-workflow; works from task/case/incident-style records
Internal-only?
No

LEAP

AIOps LEAP docs ↗

AIOps capability that finds recurring incident patterns and generates resolution steps, playbooks, and related artifacts.

Best use case
Use when the use case is recurring IT operations incidents, remediation, or playbook generation.
Customer-ready?
Yes, Store
License / entitlement
Requires ServiceNow Otto for ITOM / LEAP Store app; field material says ITOM Professional Plus or bundles including it, plus separate Playbook Assist entitlement for generating a LEAP.
Overlap / caution
Best for operational remediation and playbooks, not broad cross-workflow agentic opportunity discovery.
AI-powered?
Yes
Primary domain
ITOM / AIOps
Internal-only?
No
Intro / release signal
Q2 2025 GA materials; market launch and Store publication shown January 20, 2026 in Australia release materials.

Forge Mining

Forge Mining in the Store ↗

One-click FDE discovery accelerator that combines multiple signals into a field-ready recommendation story.

Best use case
Use when an SC or AC team wants a rapid discovery readout and business story from customer process data.
Customer-ready?
Store-listed scoped app, but field-positioned tool
License / entitlement
Not clearly documented in the sources found.
Overlap / caution
Uses mining and advisor-style signals; do not confuse it with standalone Process Mining.
AI-powered?
Partly / indirectly
Primary domain
Cross-workflow; examples seen in ITSM, HRSD, and Customer Service
Internal-only?
Mostly positioned as FDE / AC tool
